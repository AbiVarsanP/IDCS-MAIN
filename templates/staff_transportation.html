Enrollment Information

Procedure to Enroll for Transportation

1. Please fill and submit the "Online Application Form" with all the details. The Online Application Form will be enabled starting from 1st July 2025.

Note: Reg.No is a 16 digit Alpha-Numeric Number. The Login ID in the Student Copy of Admission Acknowledgement is the PRN. Please contact the admissions department if you do not have it.

2. Once the application details are verified, an E-Mail shall be sent to you on the next working day with the instruction for the online payment of the Transportation fee.

3. Payment confirmation notification will be sent after payment is verified.

4. The payment receipt (hard copy) will be issued on a particular schedule starting from 15th September 2025.

Note

  • Enrollment to the transport facility is on first come first serve basis only. No further requests will be entertained in this matter.
  • Bus fee once paid will not be refunded/adjusted under any circumstances from 1st September 2025. However during the month of August, refund is available with a 10% deduction as processing fee.
  • For the convenience of students and staff, shuttle bus service (free of cost) to the Metro Station. Timings of the pickup and drop are as below...
  • Students/ Parents can call the driver to enquire about the exact location of the pickup points.
  • The routes mentioned are final and there will not be any changes. Requests for new routes will not be entertained.
  • In case of any queries, contact the Transport In-Charge.
    Email: transport@college.edu.
    Mobile: 9113804054.
  • Payments can be made Online and by DD in favour of 'College General Fund' payable at City.
  • Cash/Cheque Not Accepted.
